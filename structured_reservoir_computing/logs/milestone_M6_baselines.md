Every prediction vector exactly reproduced its stored counterpart, and changing vibration/RPM at and after the cutoff yielded zero change through the target. Seven full-record CPU batch timings per model completed.

- Fitted nine fixed-config RPM-withholding controls on training only, then scored the common development targets. Added eight fixed-config training refits for the three-seed sensitivity of four neural families.

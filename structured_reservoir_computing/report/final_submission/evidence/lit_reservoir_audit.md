# Literature audit for the PI-SRC model class

Search conducted on 22 November 2026. This was a targeted scholarly search and comparison of methods, with reference chaining for terms with multiple established meanings. The search prioritized original articles or book chapters, supplemented by an authoritative synthesis for the definition of physics-informed learning. Published journal versions supply citation metadata. No third-party summaries establish an experimental claim in the report.

## Reconciliation and publication access

Publisher pages, indexed view excerpts and Crossref sometimes exposed different access. Crossref rate-limited some concurrent requests. DOI issuer pages or primary institutional records provided independent checks for those entries. The IEEE full-text viewer did not open in the search connector, so the journal article hosted by its Stanford author supported the Volterra explanation. The publisher page for Gauthier redirected to an identity service. Its published full text at PubMed Central supplied the actual architecture and estimator. The Elsevier reader for Racca denied a second direct fetch. Its publisher-indexed content, DOI, authoritative PubMed record and author manuscript agreed.

Local implementation claims were checked against `src/src_gearbox/src_model.py`, `docs/methodology.md`, `results/metrics/src_summary.json`, the training and comparison tables, and the diagnostic stress results. External claims in the chapter concern model design and inference scope. The local numerical evidence and scientific literature have different roles. The local evidence establishes performance, failure modes and the effect of particular feature configurations, while the literature establishes the basis and history of the methods.

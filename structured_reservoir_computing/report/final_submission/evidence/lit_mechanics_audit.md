# Mechanics and diagnostic literature audit

## Search strategy and verification

The targeted scholarly search addressed six questions. How do tooth stiffness and transmission changes create modulation? Which phase and speed conventions make an order trace interpretable? What effects do interpolation and sensor bias have on the inferred envelope? What was already implemented in published sparse time-series diagnostics? How is PMI motivated and calculated? Which claims can the project's finite, provisionally grouped records establish?

The searches used combinations of "gear tooth crack stiffness modulation McFadden", "computed order tracking Fyfe interpolation error", "gearbox angular resampling Bonnardot", "variable speed envelope Abboud speed dependence", "order tracking envelope deformation", "sparse time series gearbox baseline Chen random speed", "gearbox fluctuating novelty Schmidt", and "periodic modulation intensity Zhao Jia Chen thesis". Records were checked by DOI through Crossref for the articles and the University of Alberta's institutional ERA record for the thesis. Publisher article abstracts, accessible author manuscripts, and supplied full texts were inspected for claim scope. Publisher links returning a full-text access restriction were used only alongside registration metadata and corroborating abstracts. This is a targeted review based on the accessible primary-source evidence.

## Local-data and interpretation checks

- **Published and local scopes.** The paper's selected healthy acquisition supplied two 7.5-second segments, with the first assigned to training and the second to the modeling test. Its prose provides no exact acquisition/sample IDs that link the original test segment to the supplied course hold-out array. The paper separately uses 99 healthy and 100 crack records for experimental diagnostic testing. The current project has 200 supplied candidate records before exclusions and no verified file-to-condition mapping. Its candidate groups have 98 and 100 records after the exact overlaps are removed.
- **Physical observations.** Study-local numerical claims come from results/eda/summary.json and results/eda/extension_summary.json, including raw sample/speed bounds, training-only ridge frequencies, Welch power and the feature screen. The selected model bank and the exploratory feature-screen winners are separate model-selection results. The bounds imply 573.705 input-shaft orders at time Nyquist at the training maximum and a mesh band of 76.666–103.189 Hz.
- **Course compatibility.** The supplied fdt_featcal.m was read directly. The default search range is zero and the expression takes array element S, which corresponds to lag S−1 for autocorrelation with a lag-zero first entry. The supplied file calls toConstantSamplesPerCycle and a saved search found no instructor helper or MATLAB gold output. The project's order_tracking.py, envelope.py and pmi.py establish the implemented trapezoidal phase, 2,048 point angular grid, centered envelope, unnormalized cross-product and exact-lag alternatives. The numeric oracle and conformance caveat are recorded in results/fault/matlab_conformance.json.
- **Real candidates and synthetic interventions.** The fault status and eligibility files identify exact overlap at candidate IDs 61 and 63, no invalid retained rows and an embargo on independent health metrics. Project-local results/fault/fault_per_record.csv, fault_group_summary.csv, paired_pmi_bootstrap.csv, pulse_summary.csv and rpm_stress.csv ground the median gap, uncertainty and synthetic sensitivity claims. The bootstrap samples within provisional index groups, with acquisition linkage beyond IDs unresolved. Synthetic pulse cases evaluate preservation or distortion of a known intervention and cannot estimate field fault prevalence, specificity or a deployable threshold.
- **MSE separation.** The paper's Table 6 values, including published sparse LPV–ARMA 0.2893, are external measurements. The local report uses per-sample error in squared standardized vibration units on its documented mask. The published test window, normalization and timing setup differ or remain unresolved, and published metrics have their own label.
